# What do you do if your kid gets bullied?

2025-10-15

My wife asked me this question. Our son is 10 months old, so this isn't exactly relevant for us yet.
But she said that this question has circulated her social network groups, and that she and others
were waiting for a good answer.

I endured years of bullying as a kid, and stood up for myself violently. I trained martial arts
because I was afraid I had to defend myself, and I learned strategies through therapy late in life
that work better.

## A more important question: Is your kid getting bullied?

The person asking did have a kid who reported being bullied.

But another common scenario is that a kid gets bullied, and their parent doesn't hear about it.

Maybe some of the parents in that social network group wants to know in a future scenario when their
kid tells them they get bullied, but they never say it.

So more important than knowing what to do when your kid gets bullied: Knowing if your kid gets
bullied.
