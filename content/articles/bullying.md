## What do you do if your kid gets bullied?

This night my wife asked me this question.

Our son is 10 months old, so this isn't exactly relevant for us yet. But she said that this question
had circulated her social network groups, and that she and others were waiting for a good answer.

I endured years of bullying as a kid, and stood up for myself violently. I trained martial arts
because I was afraid I had to defend myself, and I learned strategies through therapy late in life
that work better. I vowed that I wouldn't let my kids suffer as I did, so while it has always been
in the back of my head that I need to address this, now is as good a time as ever.

I'll give the quick version in the order of importance:

1. You teach them to non-heroically but assertively stand up for themselves.
2. You teach them to tell a grown-up when they witness bullying.
3. You escalate the incident with other parents.

## A more important question: Is your kid getting bullied?

The person asking did have a kid who reported being bullied.

But another common scenario is that a kid gets bullied, and their parent doesn't hear about it.

Maybe some of the parents in that social network group wants to know in a future scenario when their
kid tells them.

More important than knowing what to do when your kid gets bullied: Knowing if your kid gets bullied.

This is more important because it is much harder to detect, and because bullying can continue
unnoticed for years.

Creating an environment where your child feels safe to tell you about being bullied comes down to
building trust and open communication over time. And it comes down to listening how their day was.
